01 / About

A writer who thinks beyond the words.

My work sits at the intersection of writing, SEO, strategy, and creative communication. I have worked with international clients as well as academic, healthcare, faith-based, and business organizations.

Strategy and storytelling, working together.

I started working in SEO in December 2022. Since then, I’ve built experience in SEO and e-commerce content, keyword and search-intent research, on-page SEO, technical SEO, Ahrefs, internal linking, backlink strategy, content planning, and content optimization.

I also work on social media management, digital communications, website content, copywriting, and creative storytelling.

How I approach SEO today.

SEO continues to evolve, especially with the rise of AI and changing search behavior. My approach stays adaptable while keeping the fundamentals strong: useful content, clear structure, accurate information, genuine search-intent alignment, topical relevance, and a strong user experience.

I aim to create content that is strategic, readable, and genuinely helpful—not generic or mechanical.
